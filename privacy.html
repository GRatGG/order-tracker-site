Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what Order Tracker collects, how it's used, and how you can remove it. If you have a question, email theclassifieddna@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your login: your email address, the display name you choose, and an optional profile picture. If you log in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your name, email address, and (from Google) profile photo.
  • Connected inboxes: when you connect a Gmail or Outlook inbox, we store its address and the access key that lets Order Tracker read it. Access keys are stored encrypted.
  • Order details: from order emails sent by online stores, we save the store name, order number, products, quantities, prices, shipping, tax, total, tracking number, carrier, status, dates, the shipping address, and which of your email addresses the order was sent to. We also save each email's ID, so the same email isn't counted twice.
  • Things you enter yourself: such as edits to orders, inventory, sales, expenses, and shipments.

We don't keep copies of your emails. Emails that aren't order emails from online stores are not saved.

How we use Google and Microsoft data

Order Tracker asks Gmail for read-only access (gmail.readonly) and Outlook for read-only mail access (Mail.Read). We use it only to find your order emails and show your orders to you. Order Tracker can't send, delete, or change your email.

Order Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Gmail data only to provide the order-tracking features you can see in the app.
  • We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, except as required by law.
  • We don't use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your email, except with your permission (for example, to help fix a problem you report), for security, or as required by law.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Only you can see your orders and data; other users can't.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect an inbox in the app's Accounts page. Order Tracker stops reading it right away.
  • Remove Order Tracker's access at any time from your Google account or Microsoft account.
  • Delete your order data in Settings → Clear all order data.
  • Delete your whole account and everything in it by emailing theclassifieddna@gmail.com. We'll do it within 30 days.

Children

Order Tracker isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
