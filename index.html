Order Tracker

Keep track of your online orders in one place.

Order Tracker finds the order emails in your inbox and turns them into a tidy list of orders. For each order it shows the store, order number, products, prices, shipping, tax, total, tracking number, status, and estimated delivery.

How it works

  • You create an Order Tracker login with an email and password, Google, or Microsoft.
  • You choose to connect a Gmail or Outlook inbox. Order Tracker asks for read-only access: it can never send, delete, or change your email.
  • It looks only for order emails from online stores (such as order confirmations and shipping updates) and saves the order details it finds. It doesn't keep copies of your emails.
  • You can disconnect an inbox at any time.

Status

Order Tracker is in early testing and isn't open for public sign-ups yet.
